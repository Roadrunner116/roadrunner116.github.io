FamilyPlanner

FamilyPlanner is a private, non-commercial wall display for a single household. It shows the family's appointments, meal plan, shopping list and household tasks in one place.

Use of Google data

FamilyPlanner reads the household's own Google Calendar with read-only access (scope calendar.readonly) to show upcoming appointments on the display. It does not create, change or delete calendar entries and does not access any other Google data.

FamilyPlanner is not offered to the public. Only the members of the household that operates it sign in.
